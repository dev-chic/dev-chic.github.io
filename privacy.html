Privacy Policy

HAI gws is a personal tool used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

  • It requests read-only access to the owner's Gmail and Google Calendar.
  • Data is processed only to generate a private briefing for the owner.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's private server and can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings.
